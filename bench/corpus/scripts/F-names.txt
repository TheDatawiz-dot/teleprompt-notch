Let me introduce the team and the stack before we get into the details.

Anirudh runs infrastructure out of Bengaluru. Siobhan handles the data
pipeline from Dublin, and Kwame joined last month to work on reliability.

We run Kubernetes on Graviton instances, with Grafana and Prometheus for
observability, and Postgres behind PgBouncer for anything transactional.

The ingestion path uses Kafka, the search layer is OpenSearch, and we use
Terraform for absolutely everything, including the things where it is
probably overkill.

Nginx sits in front, Redis caches the expensive queries, and there is a
small Rust service called Kestrel that nobody fully understands anymore.

Xiaoling wrote most of it before she moved to the platform team, and it has
not needed a change since, which tells you something.

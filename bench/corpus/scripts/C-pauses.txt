This script is about pauses. Read it with real ones.

Take a full breath here before continuing.

When you reach the end of this sentence, stop for about three seconds.

Now continue as if you had lost your train of thought and found it again.

Sometimes the most important part of speaking is the part where you say
nothing at all, and let the last sentence land before you start the next.

Pause again here. Count to four.

Then finish the script normally, at whatever pace feels natural to you.
